Join Our Team!

At Romano Roofing Co, we don’t just build roofs — we build careers. If you’re looking for a place where you can grow, be part of a positive and skilled team, and take pride in your daily work, you’ve come to the right place.

A Positive and Professional Environment

We take pride in the atmosphere we’ve created. Our team works in harmony to deliver quality roofing solutions with care, precision, and professionalism. We encourage creativity, teamwork, and a strong work ethic while fostering camaraderie and respect.

Year-Round Reliable Work

We offer more than seasonal work. Thanks to our wide range of roofing and exterior services, we provide full-time, year-round employment opportunities so our staff can rely on a stable income all year long.

Top Pay & Great Benefits

  • Top-tier pay for skilled work
  • Uniforms, boots, and seasonal gear provided
  • Healthcare benefits for office staff and project managers
  • Paid vacation days for foremen and above
  • Support for continuing education and industry certifications

We Support Our Community

We’re proud to give back — from helping neighbors after storms to supporting local causes. At Romano Roofing Co, your work makes a lasting impact on both our clients and our community.
